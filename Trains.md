## Wagons / Rolling Stock — By Cargo Type

Wagons do not vary by top speed — all wagons of a given era share the same speed limit. Instead, progression comes through:
- **Capacity** — newer wagons carry more cargo
- **Cargo Age Period** — higher = cargo ages slower in transit = more income on delivery (default is 185 ticks; values below are multipliers of default)
- **Loading Speed** — units loaded per tick; higher = faster turnaround, less time wasted at stations
