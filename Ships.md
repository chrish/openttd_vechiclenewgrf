## Ship Refit Properties

In OpenTTD, a single ship refits at a dock/depot to carry different cargoes. The tables below define how each base ship behaves per cargo type — capacity, cargo aging, and loading speed change with the refit, not by introducing separate vessels.

### General Refit Rules

| Cargo Type | Capacity Rule (from base) | Cargo Age Trend | Notes |
|-----------|--------------------------|----------------|-------|
| Passengers | Base capacity (pax ships) / ~5% of deadweight (cargo ships) | Improves with era (comfort) | Default for liners/ferries |
| Mail | ~25% of pax capacity (bags) / ~1% of deadweight (bags) | Improves moderately | Dedicated mail holds, sorted |
| Valuables | ~5% of pax capacity (bags) / ~0.2% of deadweight (bags) | Improves moderately | Secure vault hold, very limited |
| Goods | ~60% of deadweight (tonnes) | Improves with era | General break-bulk or containerised |
| Food | ~50% of goods capacity | Improves significantly (refrigeration) | Reefer holds, cold chain |
| Livestock | ~30% of goods capacity | Improves significantly (welfare) | Penned, ventilated, climate-controlled |
| Grain / Wheat | ~55% of goods capacity | Improves with era | Bulk sealed holds, dehumidified |
| Fruit / Rubber | ~45% of goods capacity | Improves significantly | Climate-managed, ethylene-scrubbed |
| Oil / Liquids | ~50% of goods capacity | 1× always | Tank compartments, hazmat rated |
| Paper | ~50% of goods capacity | Improves with era (moisture) | Covered, dehumidified holds |
| Coal | ~65% of goods capacity | 1× always | Bulk, self-trimming |
| Iron Ore | ~70% of goods capacity | 1× always | Bulk, reinforced holds (dense cargo) |
| Steel | ~40% of goods capacity | Improves slightly | Anti-corrosion, heavy-lift crane |
| Copper Ore / Water | ~60% of goods capacity | 1× always | Bulk mineral |
| Wood | ~45% of goods capacity | 1× always | Open-hatch, deck-loaded (bulky) |

*Note: Passenger ships refitted to bulk cargo carry far less than purpose-built cargo ships; cargo ships refitted to passengers carry fewer but still significant numbers.*
